On point: stand (or set the phone) on the control point, then Zero: GPS error is removed and the position is held. Sight: put the crosshair on a control point you can see, then Zero: heading (and height) are corrected. Best: occupy one point, then sight a second one.

CSTP AR CAM

Load a LandXML alignment and see it in augmented reality through your phone camera, using GPS, compass and motion sensors. Take geotagged photos with station & offset stamped on them.

This page is not served over HTTPS. Camera, GPS and compass need a secure (https://) connection.

Coordinates: Ramsey County Coordinate System (NAD83, US survey feet), same as FBK-Checker.

Tip: add to the home screen to use it full-screen and offline.
